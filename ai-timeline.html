Title

From Turing to Transformers: A Timeline of AI

A 13-slide visual timeline tracing artificial intelligence from its foundations in the 1940s and 1950s to the generative AI era. It covers the historical milestones, both AI winters (1974 to 1980 and 1987 to 1993) with their causes and impacts, the deep learning and cloud computing breakthroughs since 2012, the growth in computing power behind modern AI, and the key people and companies that shaped the field.

Objective

I created this timeline for my AIML-500 course to understand how AI evolved and to explain that history clearly to others. Many people meet AI for the first time through tools like ChatGPT and assume it appeared overnight. I wanted to show that today's AI is the result of seventy years of research, setbacks, and breakthroughs, and that understanding this history helps people judge current AI claims more realistically.

Process
  1. Research: I reviewed the course resources (an IEEE Computer Society article on the evolution of AI, Our World in Data's history of AI and its training-compute data, and a documentary video), then verified dates and facts against primary sources such as the original Turing, AlexNet, and Transformer papers.
  2. Structure: I organized the milestones into eras (foundations, the first AI winter, expert systems, the second AI winter, the groundwork of data, GPUs and cloud computing, deep learning, and generative AI) so the boom-and-bust pattern would be visible.
  3. Design: I used a consistent visual system: amber for booms, blue for winters, and teal for the data-driven era. Each event has a year, a title, and a short description. I added an overview bar of all seven decades and a chart showing that AI training compute now doubles about every six months, compared with about every 21 months before 2010.
  4. Review and refine: I checked every slide for accuracy, readability, and consistent formatting, and added a references slide and an AI-use disclosure.
Tools
Microsoft PowerPoint Claude (Anthropic) for research support and design drafting IEEE Computer Society Our World in Data Primary research papers

I used generative AI as a research and drafting assistant and verified its output against the sources listed below.

Value
For leaders and teams adopting AI, this timeline is a five-minute, evidence-based briefing on how AI got here. It helps them separate lasting breakthroughs from hype, understand why past AI booms collapsed when promises outran technology, and plan AI adoption with realistic expectations.
Key insights
  • Progress came when theory, data, and hardware aligned. The 2012 AlexNet breakthrough combined neural network theory, the ImageNet dataset, and Nvidia GPUs.
  • Both AI winters followed overpromising, a lesson that still applies to today's generative AI hype.
  • Cloud computing and GPUs turned AI from a lab experiment into an industry by making massive computing power available to rent.
